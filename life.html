Japan Trip 2024

Next journey ↓

In the summer of 2024, I had a trip to Japan with my best friends in high school. It is my first time to go abroad. Landing in Osaka , then we rode the train "haruka" to Kyoto directly and stayed there for 4 days. After that, we made our way to Kanazawa, Ishigawa , where we watched a gorgeous sunset besides the Japan sea. Finally, we took Shinkansen to Tokyo , experienced a modern city life of Japan, shopping around, watching a band live, having yummy food and so on.
It is true that Japan culture made great influence in my life. Those books, anime helped through a tough time in my high school. So when I first stepped onto that land, I felt so excited and cured.
Here, I would like to share with you a lot of funny stories we experienced.

Europe Solo Trip 2025

Life back home ↓

In the summer of 2025, I embarked on the first solo-trip of my life, an experience I will never forget. Beginning in Stockholm, Sweden , where I spent most of the summer on a research internship there, I made my way through Denmark, Germany, the Netherlands, France, Switzerland , finally to Italy .
Along the way, I encountered breathtaking landscapes and experienced cultures strikingly different from those I had known in China. In many ways, this journey might shape the next decade of my life.
During this trip, I also began to view about my childhood from a completely new perspective. Many of the questions that had puzzled me for years seemed to find their answers as I traveled. Here, I would like to share these with you, together with many pictures I took.
